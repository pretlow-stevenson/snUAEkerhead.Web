About

Presence and control.

We began with sneakers and the way a single pair can change an outfit. That eye for personal style now shapes our approach to apparel, typography and art.

The UAE is part of our name and the foundation of our identity. Travel expands that perspective. We interpret each new reference through our own experiences and design sensibility.

In our work, presence comes from a silhouette or graphic that holds its own. Control is knowing how much it needs: the scale of a mark, the shape of a collar, the space around a line of type.

We carry that thinking into garment research, from shoulder placement and sleeve volume to the way fabric feels and falls.

“The garment comes first. The artwork follows.”

Streetwear leaves room for personal interpretation through footwear, silhouette, layering and unexpected combinations. We welcome everyone who finds a connection with our work.

Exploration

What we bring back.

Travel changes what we notice: how an outfit comes together, a building’s proportions, the care in an object’s presentation. We bring those observations into our design process.

Dubai’s architecture and hospitality sharpened our eye for presentation and execution. In Tokyo, we learned from the way people combined wide trousers, boxy T-shirts and distinctive footwear. Muted colour brought drape, collar shape and the proportions of an outfit into focus.

“The color is the canvas. The design is the paint.”

Black, charcoal and natural tones give us room to work with shape, lettering and space. A small gold accent draws the eye to a detail.

Those observations are also shaping our developing Travel Series. Its first concept, Tokyo, connects our UAE identity with our experience of Japan through Arabic and Japanese lettering, apparel graphics and label treatments.
