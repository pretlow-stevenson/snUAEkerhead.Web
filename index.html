About

An independent point of view.

snUAEkerhead is an independent streetwear brand rooted in the United Arab Emirates. Sneaker culture is the starting point; exploration opens the perspective.

Experiences in Dubai and Tokyo have shaped our founder’s attention to proportion, texture, typography and personal style.

Presence and control guide the work: expressive design, considered details and room for individual interpretation.
