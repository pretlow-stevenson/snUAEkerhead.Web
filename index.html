About

Presence and control.

We began with sneaker culture and an interest in how people make clothing their own. Rooted in the United Arab Emirates, we explore that connection through apparel, typography and art.

A distinct foundation

The UAE is part of our name and the foundation of our identity. Our time in Dubai has shaped how we think about confidence, presentation and detail. Its architecture, fashion and international energy remain important references in our work.

Travel brings new perspectives. We interpret them through our own experiences and design sensibility, with the UAE always at the centre.

A considered approach

Presence is the confidence of a silhouette or graphic that holds its own. Control is the judgement behind its scale, spacing, colour and placement. Together, they give our work its direction.

With a restrained palette, shape and texture carry more of the expression. Typography, proportion and the space around an element all contribute to how a design is read.

The garment itself

Our attention begins with fit: the balance of the body and sleeves, shoulder position, collar shape and the way fabric falls. Artwork belongs within that whole.

We want each garment to have a character of its own. Materials, construction and finishing are priorities as we develop our designs.

Individual expression

Streetwear leaves room for personal interpretation through footwear, silhouette, layering and unexpected combinations. We welcome everyone who finds a connection with our work. Each person brings their own experiences and identity to what they wear.

Exploration

Travel as a creative practice.

We travel with an eye for how people dress and how places take shape. An outfit, a building, a carefully presented object: each can offer a new way to think about proportion, colour and composition.

Dubai: presence and presentation

Dubai has shaped our relationship with the UAE and our understanding of presence. Its architecture, international culture and attention to presentation continue to influence how we think about confidence and detail.

Our experiences in the city’s hospitality and dining sharpened an appreciation for considered service and precise execution. We carry that attention to detail into how we develop and present our work.

Tokyo: proportion and individuality

September 2026

In Tokyo, we explored fashion and retail across Shibuya, Harajuku, Marunouchi and Otemachi. Shibuya PARCO and vintage retailer Desert Snow were among our stops. Just as much came from noticing how people wore and combined their clothes.

Wide trousers, boxy T-shirts, layering and distinctive footwear caught our attention. Muted colours often allowed shape, texture and proportion to carry the visual interest.

Those encounters sharpened our interest in drape, collar construction and the relationship between restraint and personal style.

Colour, silhouette and detail

One thought stayed with us:

“The color is the canvas. The design is the paint.”

Black, charcoal and natural tones give us a foundation for exploring shape, lettering and space. A small gold accent can bring a particular detail into focus. Each colour has a role within the composition.

A continuing practice

The journey continues through photography, garment research and the refinement of artwork. Tokyo is the first concept in our developing Travel Series, exploring connections between the UAE and Japan through apparel graphics and label treatments.

Every chapter begins with experience and research, giving us something meaningful to carry into the work.
